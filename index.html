How it works

  1. Select the region to watch, right on your live screen.
  2. Select the point to click when it changes.
  3. Tune sensitivity — react to a flicker, or only to something big.
  4. Press Start. ScreenWatch clicks the instant the region changes, and logs why.
What are all the features?

Live, real-screen selection

Drag directly on your real, moving desktop — the screen is never covered while you select.

Colour-aware detection

Reacts to hue changes, not just brightness, with noise filtering.

Two detection modes

React to any change, or only a deviation from a fixed starting state.

See exactly why it clicked

Every click is logged with a picture of the pixels that triggered it, browsable per click.

Customizable hotkeys

Start, stop, and quit with global shortcuts you record yourself.

Built for long runs

Down-sampled comparisons keep it under 1% CPU for a multi-hour session.
